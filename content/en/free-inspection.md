## Why Get a Free Inspection?

### After a Storm
Wilmington and Brunswick County face hurricanes, tropical storms, and severe thunderstorms regularly. High winds lift shingles and loosen flashing even when damage isn't visible from the ground. An inspection catches issues before they become leaks.

### Before Buying or Selling
Buyers learn exactly what they're getting into. Sellers can address issues before listing and avoid surprises during negotiations. A professional inspection report protects both sides.

### Routine Maintenance
Small issues caught early cost $150-$400 to fix. The same issue ignored for months can cause $5,000+ in interior water damage. Annual inspections are the cheapest insurance you can buy for your roof.
