## Types of Storm Damage We Repair

### Hurricane Damage
Brunswick County sits in the direct path of Atlantic hurricanes. 100+ mph winds can tear off shingles, lift roof sections, and cause structural damage.
- Partial or complete roof blow-off
- Widespread shingle displacement
- Damage from fallen trees
- Water intrusion throughout the home

### Wind Damage
Tropical storms and severe thunderstorms produce damaging winds across the region.
- Lifted or missing shingles
- Loosened flashing
- Flying debris damage

### Hail Damage
Hail dents and cracks shingles — damage that often shows up as leaks months later.

### Water Damage
Damaged roofs let water ruin interiors: insulation, drywall, flooring, and belongings. Quick action is critical.
