## Seamless vs Sectional Gutters: Why We Only Install Seamless

Sectional gutters from big-box stores join 10-foot pieces with seams every few feet — and every seam is a future leak. Wilmington's heat cycles expand and contract those joints until they drip directly onto your fascia.

Seamless gutters are roll-formed on-site to your home's exact measurements in one continuous run. Fewer joints means fewer failure points — critical here where a single dripping seam over one summer can rot a fascia board and invite termites.

## Gutter Guards: Are They Worth It in Coastal NC?

If your yard has longleaf pine, live oaks, or cypress — you already know the answer. Pine needles slip through most guards, and oak tassels in spring clog cheap mesh within weeks.

What actually works in Wilmington:
- **Micro-mesh with fine perforations** — blocks pine needles, not just leaves
- **Properly pitched installation** — debris sheds off instead of damming
- **Professional annual inspection** — no guard eliminates maintenance entirely; anyone claiming otherwise is selling you a problem for later

Homes in Ogden, Porters Neck, and Masonboro with heavy canopy coverage typically cut gutter cleaning from twice a year to once every year-or-two after guard installation. See our dedicated [gutter guards page](/gutter-guards-shallotte-nc) for options and pricing.

## Signs You Need Gutter Repair or Replacement

Call us if you notice any of these around your Wilmington home:
1. **Water spilling over the front edge** during normal rain — clog or improper pitch
2. **Gutters pulling away from fascia** — failed spikes/hangers, common after storm wind
3. **Rust spots or orange streaking** — coating failure, corrosion accelerating in salt air
4. **Peeling paint or water marks below gutters** — slow leak behind the fascia
5. **Basement or crawlspace moisture** after storms — downspouts discharging too close to foundation
6. **Landscape trenches** under drip lines — uncontrolled runoff eroding beds

Repairs caught early cost a fraction of replacing rotted fascia, soffit, or repairing a foundation later.
