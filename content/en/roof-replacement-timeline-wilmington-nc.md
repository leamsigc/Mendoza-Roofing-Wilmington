**The short answer:** a residential roof replacement in Wilmington, NC usually takes **1 to 3 days of installation**. A small, simple shingle roof can be done in about a day; an average home takes 1–3 days; large, steep, or complex roofs take 3–5 days or more. The wait from signed contract to start is often 1–4 weeks.

The installation is the short part. Scheduling, materials, permits, weather, and hidden deck damage are what turn a “two-day job” into a two-week project.

## Installation time by roof type

| Roof | Installation time | What drives it |
|---|---|---|
| Small, simple asphalt shingle roof | About 1 day | Straight runs, easy access, one layer |
| Average single-family home | 1 – 3 days | Tear-off, underlayment, shingles, flashing, cleanup |
| Large or two-story home | 2 – 4 days | More square footage, staging, multiple roof planes |
| Steep, cut-up roof with dormers and valleys | 3 – 5+ days | Safety, detail work, flashing, valleys |
| Tile, slate or complex metal roof | 5 – 10+ days | Specialized installation, heavier materials, detail work |
| Commercial low-slope roof | 1 – 3+ weeks | Phasing, membrane, insulation, drainage, occupied building |

A one-day roof is not automatically better than a three-day roof. Speed matters less than a dry, correctly flashed, cleanly finished roof.

## The full timeline, from call to cleanup

### 1. Inspection and estimate (1–3 days)

A free inspection should include the roof surface, flashing, attic if accessible, and photos. You receive a written scope and price. If the roof is storm-damaged, the inspection may also document damage for insurance.

### 2. Contract and material selection (1–5 days)

Choose the shingle or metal color and confirm the scope. Material lead times vary; common architectural shingles are usually in stock locally, while specialty colors, tile, and standing-seam panels may take longer.

### 3. Permit and scheduling (1–10 business days)

Some jurisdictions require a roofing permit. The contractor should handle it. Scheduling depends on crew availability, season, and weather. Spring and fall are busy; after a named storm, schedules tighten.

### 4. Tear-off and deck inspection (same day to 1 day)

The old roof comes off and the deck is inspected. Rotten sheathing or fascia is replaced at the agreed unit rate. This is the most common source of a schedule change.

### 5. Installation (1–3 days typical)

Underlayment, flashing, shingles or panels, ridge caps, vents, and cleanup. A crew should make the roof watertight before leaving each day.

### 6. Final inspection and cleanup (same day)

Walk the roof, check flashing and penetrations, magnet-sweep the yard, and remove the dumpster. You should receive photos and warranty documents.

## What delays a Wilmington roof replacement

- **Rain and wind.** Crews cannot install in high wind or heavy rain. An open roof must be protected, and a week of weather can push the schedule.
- **Hidden deck damage.** Soft sheathing, rotted fascia, or water-damaged framing adds time and cost.
- **Multiple layers.** Two or three old roofs take longer to tear off and increase disposal costs.
- **Roof complexity.** Dormers, valleys, skylights, chimneys, and steep pitches slow installation.
- **Material availability.** Specialty colors, tile, and metal panels may need to be ordered.
- **Permits and inspections.** Required inspections can add a day or more.
- **Crew size.** A two-person crew on a large roof takes longer than a full crew, even if the daily rate looks lower.

## Hurricane season timing

Wilmington's hurricane season runs June through November. If you schedule a replacement during that window, ask:

- How will you protect the roof if a storm develops mid-project?
- What is the maximum area you will leave open overnight?
- Do you have priority access to materials if a storm hits?
- How do you handle a storm during the warranty period?

A contractor who cannot answer those questions is not planning for the coast.

## What you can do to keep the schedule on track

1. Move vehicles and clear the driveway before the crew arrives.
2. Trim branches that block access or rub the roof.
3. Protect attic items under the work area from dust.
4. Decide on material and color before the start date.
5. Keep children and pets away from the work zone.
6. Be available for deck-damage approval decisions.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a roof replacement take in Wilmington, NC?

A residential roof replacement in Wilmington, NC usually takes one to three days of installation. A small, simple shingle roof can be done in about one day, an average home takes one to three days, and large, steep or complex roofs take three to five days or more. The wait from signed contract to start is often one to four weeks.

### Does rain delay a roof replacement?

Yes. Crews cannot install shingles or membrane safely in high wind or heavy rain, and an open roof must be protected. A good contractor phases the work, watches the forecast, and makes the roof watertight before leaving each day. A short delay is normal; a week of rain can push the schedule.

### Can I stay in my house during a roof replacement?

In most cases yes. You may hear noise, feel vibration, and need to move vehicles out of the driveway. The contractor should protect landscaping, cover attic items if needed, and clean up nails daily. If the roof is being fully torn off, ask how they will protect the interior if rain arrives.
