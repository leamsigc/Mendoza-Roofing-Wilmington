## The Ogden Roof We See Most: The Original 20-Year Shingle

Most Ogden homes were built in the 1980s through 2000s, and a large share still wear their original builder-grade three-tab shingles. That roof is at or past its design life right now. The tells, in the order they usually appear:

1. **Granules in the gutters** — the protective mineral layer is shedding; what's left is asphalt and mat
2. **Curling or cupping edges** — the shingle is drying out and losing its seal
3. **A wavy roofline** — decking underneath is starting to swell and sag
4. **The first leak** — by the time water shows inside, decking damage is usually already done

If your home is showing #1 or #2, you have time to plan a replacement on your schedule and your budget. If you're at #4, call us today — active leaks get same-day response, and we'll tell you honestly whether a repair buys you a season or it's time for the full job.
