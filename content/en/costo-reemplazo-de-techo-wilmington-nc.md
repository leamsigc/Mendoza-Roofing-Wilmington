## What Affects Roof Replacement Cost in Wilmington?

### Roof Size and Pitch
Bigger roofs cost more — that's obvious. But **steep roofs** cost significantly more because they require extra safety equipment, more labor time, and specialized installation techniques. A 2-story home with a steep pitch can cost 20-40% more than a single-story home with the same square footage.

### Number of Existing Layers
If your home has **two or more layers** of old roofing, tear-off costs increase. Each additional layer adds $1,000-$3,000 to the project. Most Wilmington homes have 1-2 layers.

### Deck Condition
When we remove your old roof, we inspect the wood deck underneath. **Rotted or damaged decking** requires replacement — typically $500-$2,000 depending on extent. This can't be known until the old roof is removed, which is why written estimates include a decking allowance.

### Coastal Building Code Requirements
Wilmington and Brunswick County enforce **stricter building codes** than inland NC:
- Wind-rated shingles (130+ mph minimum) — adds $500-$1,500
- Enhanced fastening patterns — adds $300-$800
- Ice and water barrier at eaves and valleys — adds $400-$800
- Corrosion-resistant flashing and fasteners — adds $200-$500

These aren't optional — they're code requirements. Any contractor bidding significantly below these ranges is likely cutting corners.

### Material Choice
This is the biggest cost driver. Here's the trade-off:

**Asphalt shingles** = lower upfront cost, shorter lifespan. You'll replace them 2-3 times over the life of a metal roof.

**Metal roofing** = 2-3x the upfront cost, but lasts 2-3x longer. Over 50 years, metal often costs less per year.

**Tile roofing** = premium cost, premium lifespan. Best for high-end homes where aesthetics matter.
