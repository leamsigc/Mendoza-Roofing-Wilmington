## Signs You Need a Roofer Near You Today

Some roof problems can wait a week. These cannot:

- **Water dripping inside the house** — active leak, call immediately.
- **Ceiling stains or bubbling paint** — water is already inside the assembly.
- **Missing or lifted shingles after wind** — the next rain gets under the field.
- **Granules in gutters or at downspouts** — shingles are near end of life.
- **Daylight visible in the attic** — a penetration or ridge failure.
- **Sagging roofline** — possible decking or structural damage.
- **Lifted, cracked, or missing flashing** around chimneys and skylights.

If you see any of these, [request a free inspection](/free-roof-inspection-wilmington-nc) before the next storm system moves in.

## Repair or Replace? The Honest Test

| Situation | Better choice |
|---|---|
| Roof under 15 years old, one isolated leak | Repair |
| A few damaged shingles after wind | Repair |
| Failed flashing or boot around a penetration | Repair |
| Roof 20+ years old with widespread granule loss | Replacement |
| Multiple leaks in different areas | Replacement |
| Sagging decking or repeated repairs in the same spot | Replacement |

We do both, so the recommendation is not driven by what we prefer to sell. If a repair will realistically buy you five more years, we will say so. Compare options on our [repair vs. replacement guide](/blog/roof-repair-vs-replacement-wilmington-nc).
