## Our Roofing Services

### Roof Replacement
We replace entire roofs with asphalt shingles, metal, tile, and flat systems. Includes tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and installation to NC wind codes.

### Roof Repair
We fix leaks, damaged shingles, flashing, and storm damage before they become major problems.

### Metal Roofing
Metal is ideal for the coast: it lasts 40-70 years, resists hurricanes and salt air. We offer corrugated and standing seam metal.

### FORTIFIED Roofs
We install FORTIFIED-certified roofs that meet IBHS wind standards and may qualify for up to $6,000 in grant funding.

### Storm Damage Repair
24/7 response to hurricane, wind, and hail damage with full insurance claim support.

### Gutters
We install seamless gutters and gutter protection across the region.
