> This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. Have a qualified attorney review it before treating it as final.

## What we collect

- **Contact form:** your name, email address and message — only what you type in. We use it to respond to your estimate request and nothing else.
- **Phone/email:** if you call [910-367-7628](tel:9103677628) or write to [contact@roofingmendoza.com](mailto:contact@roofingmendoza.com), we keep the conversation so we can schedule and document your project.
- **Analytics:** privacy-friendly, aggregate visit statistics (via Umami) with no cross-site tracking and no sale of data.
- **Preferences:** a cookie remembering your language choice (`i18n_redirected`) and theme preference.

## What we never do

- We never sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.
- We never add you to a marketing list you did not ask to join.

## How long we keep it

Estimate requests are kept for the duration of your project plus standard business record-keeping, then deleted on request.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it: [contact@roofingmendoza.com](mailto:contact@roofingmendoza.com), [910-367-7628](tel:9103677628), P.O. Box 952, Supply, NC 28462.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
