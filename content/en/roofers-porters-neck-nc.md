## What Porters Neck Roofs Need That Inland Roofs Don't

**Salt-air-rated flashing and fasteners.** Standard galvanized flashing near the ICW rusts through in 10–15 years. We use properly coated metals and stainless where it matters — the difference between a flashing that lasts the roof's life and one that starts the leak cycle early.

**Wind-rated attachment.** Coastal New Hanover County sees higher design wind speeds than inland areas. Shingle choice matters less than what holds it down: correct underlayment, sealed edges, and fastening patterns rated for the gusts this peninsula actually gets.

**HOA-ready documentation.** Porters Neck is a gated community with architectural standards. We bring product sheets and color samples for approval, then install to manufacturer spec so your warranty survives the HOA process.
