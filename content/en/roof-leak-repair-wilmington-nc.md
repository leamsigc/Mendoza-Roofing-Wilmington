## Where Roof Leaks Hide in Wilmington Homes

Coastal North Carolina roofs fail in predictable places. Here's what we find most often — and fix:

| Leak Source | Why It Fails Here | Typical Fix |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Pipe boots & vent flashings** | Rubber gaskets crack after 8–12 years of UV and salt air | Replace boot, $150–$350 |
| **Step & counter flashing** | Corrosion from salt air; loose after wind events | Reline or replace, $300–$800 |
| **Valleys** | Debris dams force water sideways under shingles | Clean, reflash, reshingle |
| **Wind-lifted shingles** | Coastal gusts break the shingle seal | Re-seal or replace course |
| **Skylights** | Old seals and improper curb flashing | Reflash or replace unit |
| **Chimneys** | Cracked mortar caps and failed flashing | Tuckpoint + new flashing |
| **Flat roof seams** | TPO/EPDM seams open with thermal cycling | Re-weld or patch |

## Leak Repair vs. Full Replacement?

A honest question worth asking: is this leak a $300 fix or the first symptom of an aging roof?

Rules of thumb we use after 50 years:

- **Under 10 years old** → almost always worth repairing
- **15–20 years, isolated leak** → repair makes sense
- **20+ years, multiple leaks, curling shingles everywhere** → replacement is usually cheaper than chasing leaks every season
- **Storm damage across large sections** → often fully covered by insurance

Our inspectors will tell you straight which situation you're in — see our guide on [roof repair vs replacement](/blog/roof-repair-vs-replacement-wilmington-nc), or check current [Wilmington NC roof replacement costs](/roof-replacement-cost-wilmington-nc).
