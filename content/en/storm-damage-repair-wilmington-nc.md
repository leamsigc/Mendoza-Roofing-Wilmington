## Types of Storm Damage We Repair in Wilmington

### Hurricane & Tropical Storm Damage
Hurricanes bring 100+ mph winds that can lift shingles, blow off roof sections, and push water through even minor vulnerabilities.
- Widespread shingle displacement
- Partial or complete roof blow-off
- Flashing and vent damage
- Water intrusion throughout the home

### High Wind Damage
Severe thunderstorms and tropical squalls routinely produce damaging winds across Wilmington.
- Lifted or curled shingles
- Loosened flashing around chimneys and skylights
- Debris impact damage

### Hail Damage
Hailstones dent shingles, crack ridge caps, and bruise metal roofing — damage that often shows up as leaks months later.
- Granule loss exposing the mat
- Cracked or split shingles
- Dented metal panels and gutters

### Falling Tree & Debris Damage
Coastal storms routinely bring down trees and limbs onto roofs and structures.
